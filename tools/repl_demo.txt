# tools/repl_demo.txt — a scripted session; run with:  build/release/repl < tools/repl_demo.txt
add ACME
sell ACME 100 10.02 as 2
sell ACME 50 10.02 as 3
sell ACME 70 10.01 as 4
buy ACME 30 9.99 as 5
book ACME
# crosses 10.01 first (better price), then 10.02 oldest-first
buy ACME 150 10.02 as 6
book ACME
# a market sell sweeps the bids; the remainder is cancelled, never rests
msell ACME 40 as 7
# order #1 belongs to participant 2: anyone else's cancel is refused
cancel 1 as 9
cancel 1 as 2
# participant 3 buying into its own resting ask: that ask is cancelled instead
buy ACME 10 10.02 as 3
book ACME
# an amend: smaller at the same price keeps its place in the queue...
sell ACME 60 10.05 as 2
sell ACME 40 10.05 as 4
amend 8 10.05 20 as 2
# ...and a new price sends it to the back, where it can trade like a new order
amend 8 10.04 20 as 2
book ACME
# an auction: orders collect without matching, so the book may cross...
auction ACME
buy ACME 30 10.06 as 5
buy ACME 25 10.05 as 6
sell ACME 20 10.03 as 7
book ACME
# ...until the uncross matches them all at the one price that trades the most
uncross ACME
book ACME
